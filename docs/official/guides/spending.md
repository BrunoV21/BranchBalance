# Plan and understand group spending

The **Spending** tab answers how much the group spent, where the money went, and how that compares with the group’s plan.

## Trip groups

A Trip can have start and end dates, a total budget, and optional category limits. When the dates and total budget are set, BranchBalance shows how much remains and simple daily guidance for the rest of the trip.

The pace line is a reference for spreading the budget evenly—not a prediction of future spending.

## Fuel groups

A Fuel group can have a spending limit for each calendar month. Its overview and spending screens show fuel purchases, litres, average paid price, discounts, and stations when those details were recorded.

Missing fuel details are shown as incomplete and are never guessed.

## Categories and payment methods

Expenses include a category and Card, Cash, or Other payment method. Older expenses without those details remain visible as **Uncategorized** or **Unspecified**.

## Filters

Combine category, payment method, payer, and Shared-versus-Just-me filters to find the expenses behind a total. Filters only change what you are viewing; they do not change balances or budgets.
