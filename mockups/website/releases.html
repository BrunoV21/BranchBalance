What is new in v1.2.0

This release adds private on-device receipt scanning with an editable review before anything is saved.

Highlights

Private receipt scanning

Read receipt photos on your phone and review every detected detail before saving.

You stay in control

Choose the category, payment method, payer, and split instead of letting a scan decide.

Easy to correct

Edit uncertain values, retake the photo, or continue with manual entry at any time.

Existing groups still work

Your groups, expenses, payments, activity, and spending views remain compatible.

Before you update

  • Your existing groups and expenses continue to work; there is nothing to migrate.
  • Android is currently the only supported platform.
  • Saving shared changes still requires an internet connection.
  • Receipt recognition happens on your phone; saving the confirmed expense still needs an internet connection.
Already using BranchBalance?

Install v1.2.0 over your current copy. Android keeps your local sign-in and cached data when the update uses the same official signing key.

Technical release details

The release includes signed 64-bit and 32-bit Android APKs plus a SHA-256 checksum for people who want to verify either file.
