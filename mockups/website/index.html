Getting started

From download to your first shared expense.

Install the Android app

Download BranchBalance directly from this website and follow the short Android installation guide.

Sign in securely

Continue to GitHub with an existing account or create a free one. BranchBalance explains the requested access first.

Create or join

Start a private group and invite people you know, or accept an invitation to an existing group.

Add and settle

Record expenses as they happen. Everyone sees updated spending and a clear list of balances.

Private without the mystery

Your group controls its history.

BranchBalance uses a private space in GitHub for your group. That means the creator controls access, the data can be exported, and BranchBalance does not keep a second copy on its own server.

Only invited people

The group creator chooses who can open and change the shared expenses.

Exportable history

Your records are readable and can be downloaded instead of being trapped inside the app.

No advertising profile

BranchBalance does not use group spending to serve ads or build a marketing profile.
